Stops at the first bypass and records the winning mode.

⚠ Cost warning: Worst-case: 17 LLM calls per prompt (16 modes + baseline). Early-stop on first bypass means most probes resolve in 1–3 calls against a known model.
Ollama concurrency capped at 3 simultaneous requests.
